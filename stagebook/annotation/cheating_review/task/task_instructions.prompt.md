## About this task

Below are the recordings for one group. There are **six rounds** (1a, 1b, 1c, 2, 3, 4) and one clip per round, showing both participants together. You can scrub, pause, and change playback speed.

You do **not** need to watch every clip end-to-end. Skim, sample, and focus in wherever something looks off. The goal is to form an overall judgment about whether the group cheated.

After the clips, mark your judgment and (if relevant) briefly describe what you saw.
