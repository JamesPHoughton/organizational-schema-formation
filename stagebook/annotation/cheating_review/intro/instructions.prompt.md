# Cheating review

You will review the webcam recordings from **one group** of two participants who completed a coordination study.

The group played **six rounds** of a naming-and-recall task. Each round had two stages:

1. **Discussion** — the two participants talked over video and assigned a unique label to each of a small set of images. **The recordings you'll see are from this stage only.**
2. **Recall** — each participant, individually and silently, typed in the label they remembered for each image. The participants could not see or hear each other during this stage, and it is not recorded.

We are using this annotation to decide whether a group should be **excluded from analysis** for cheating. After watching the recordings, you will be asked to give a single judgment about the group as a whole, and optionally describe what you observed.

Please read the cheating criteria carefully on the next page before you start watching.
